# Posting schedule: 3 a day (times in US Eastern; adjust once Studio shows where viewers are)

| Slot | Time (ET) | What | Why this time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12:00 PM** | New full episode (2-3 min) | 5 PM UTC was the strongest hour in PostFast's 6,175-post study (1.29x); 1 PM audience time 1.25x |
| 2 | **4:30 PM** | Teaser A: the cliffhanger moment (under 60s) | Buffer (1.8M videos) and PostEverywhere both rank 4-7 PM top; avoid 6 PM (0.80x in PostFast) |
| 3 | **8:30 PM** | Teaser B: the hook / emotional moment (under 60s) | 8-9 PM scored 1.12-1.19x in PostFast |

Friday 4-7 PM was the single best window in two studies, so put the strongest teaser in Friday's slot 2.

## Daily production (automated)
Each morning the production run makes the next episode: script (hook, then backstory, a turn, the reveal, a cliffhanger and a comment prompt), realistic voice, 15-18 animated scenes, thumbnail, upload kit, and two teasers. You get the files on your phone with the kit, ready to schedule.

## Weekly review
Every Sunday: read each video's stats (views, average % viewed, the retention graph, comments). Then
- fix what's losing people (the first 3 seconds, a slow scene, a confusing beat),
- double down on the hooks and moments that held viewers,
- pick the best viewer theory to read in the next episode.
