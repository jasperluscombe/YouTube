# Posting schedule: one new story a day, in 3 parts (US Eastern)

| Slot | Time (ET) | What | Why this time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12:00 PM** | Part 1/3: hook + setup, ends on a cliffhanger | 5 PM UTC was the strongest hour in PostFast's 6,175-post study (1.29x); 1 PM audience time 1.25x |
| 2 | **4:30 PM** | Part 2/3: escalation + twist, ends on a cliffhanger | Buffer (1.8M videos) and PostEverywhere both rank 4-7 PM top; avoid 6 PM (0.80x in PostFast) |
| 3 | **8:30 PM** | Part 3/3: the finale + "new story tomorrow" | 8-9 PM scored 1.12-1.19x in PostFast |

Every part: 150-175s, title format `<Story> (1/3): <name> #shorts`, a "1/3" label in the first seconds,
and a comment prompt on the end card. Different story every day (not one long series).

## Nightly production (automated, 11:23 PM ET)
Writes, voices, animates and assembles the next day's 3 parts + thumbnails + upload kit.
Uploads and schedules them itself once YouTube is connected (launch/CONNECT_YOUTUBE.md);
until then it sends you the files to schedule in the YouTube app.

## Weekly review (Sundays 7:23 PM ET)
Looks at the numbers: which stories and parts held viewers, where they dropped off, and how many
Part 1 viewers came back for Parts 2 and 3. It writes up the changes and applies them to the next stories.
