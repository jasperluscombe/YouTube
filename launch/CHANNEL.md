# Channel setup: Last Light Stories

**Name:** Last Light Stories
**Handle:** @LastLightStories (fallbacks: @LastLightTales, @LastLightAnimated)
**Profile picture:** launch/avatar.png
**Banner:** launch/banner.png (2560x1440; the text sits inside the safe area for all devices)

**Description (paste into Customize channel > Basic info):**
> The world went quiet. You didn't.
> Animated survival mysteries, told one episode at a time: empty cities, strange signals, and the dog who never left your side.
> New episodes every day at 12 PM ET, with extra clips at 4:30 PM and 8:30 PM.
> Comment your theories. The best ones get read in the next episode.

**Channel keywords:** animated story, survival story, mystery, apocalypse, last man on earth, empty city, liminal, story time animation, what if, sci fi shorts

## Create it (about 2 minutes, in the YouTube app)
1. Tap your profile picture > **Your channel** (or **Create a channel**). Enter the name **Last Light Stories** and the handle.
2. Tap the pencil > upload `avatar.png` as the picture and `banner.png` as the banner. Paste the description.
3. Settings > Channel > Advanced (in YouTube Studio): audience = **No, it's not made for kids**.
