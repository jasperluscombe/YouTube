## What the numbers say
1. **Best story: the zombie pharmacy** (most views, most reactions, and the best single watch time: 14.1 s on
   TikTok Part 1). Worst: The Last Gondola, especially on YouTube (286).
2. **Returning viewers**: on TikTok, Parts 2-3 get 30-110% of Part 1's views depending on the story (Lights 31%,
   Lighthouse ~100%, Pharmacy 78-112%, Gondola 20-35%). Parts are being served as separate videos to new people more
   than they are followed as a series. Each part must work alone.
3. **YouTube rewards a Part 2 whose description states a hard fact** (Lighthouse P2 526, Hear That? P2 518, Pharmacy
   P2 358) - three stories in a row.
4. **The fast format has NOT shown a win yet.** Day 1 at 1.3 days: 1,258 views. At a similar age the pharmacy story
   had ~2,200, Hear That? ~1,450, Gondola ~940. So it sits mid-pack: better than the last 2-minute Kai story, below
   the best. Instagram is its best platform ever (595; Part 3 alone 347); TikTok is its worst (275).
5. **The real problem is unchanged: people leave at 4-9 seconds whatever the length.** Day 1's average watch is
   3.9-8.4 s, the same seconds as the 2-minute videos. Shorter videos raise the percentage watched but nobody new
   is staying past the opening line. The first 3 seconds decide everything, and ours still spend them on a
   6-second spoken sentence plus a text card.
6. Time slots: no slot is consistently best. 12:00 PM Part 1s lead on TikTok; 4:30 PM Part 2s lead on YouTube.
   Not enough data to move the schedule.

## Changes applied (from the next story made)
- **Hook in 3 seconds**: the first spoken line is at most 8 words and under 3 seconds, with the most dangerous image
  of the part already moving in frame 1 ("Never drink seawater." / "Forty infected. One glass door."). The longer
  "If..., don't..." decision comes second.
- **No "ZOMBIE OUTBREAK 1/3" label bar in the first 4 seconds** on fast-format parts (it tells a new viewer this is
  a fragment before they are hooked). The part number stays in the title and the end card.
- Keep: zombie survival, real facts stated plainly in Part 2-3 descriptions, fast format for at least 4 more days
  before judging it (one story is not a test), character voices.
- Judge every story at 2+ days old: Hear That? went from 334 to 1,162 on TikTok after day 1.
