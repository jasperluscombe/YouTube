Facts checked for Day 2 (The Rescue Boat):
- Seawater is ~3.5% salt; drinking it adds to the body's salt load and worsens dehydration. Rain is the fallback;
  let the first rain rinse sea-spray salt off the collecting surface.
  Sources: [Popular Science](https://www.popsci.com/article/technology/lost-sea-survive-these-tricks/),
  [University of Portsmouth sea-survival expert](https://csd.docstore.port.ac.uk/news-events-and-blogs/blogs/human-experience/surf-group-found-safe-after-days-at-sea-in-indonesia-a-sea-survival-expert-on-what-it-takes-to-survive-being-lost-at-sea),
  [OffGrid](https://www.offgridweb.com/survival/what-if-youre-lost-at-sea/amp/)
- A flash of reflected/emitted light can be seen for miles; sources disagree on how well a mirror works among sea
  glare, so the script uses a phone screen at sunset and "three flashes" (three of anything = distress) and makes no
  distance claim. Sources: [SOFREP](https://sofrep.com/news/combat-the-ocean-military-grade-survival-tips-when-stranded-in-the-deep), [The Yacht Market](https://www.theyachtmarket.com/en/articles/general/survival-at-sea/)
- Trouser flotation (knot the legs, fill with air, hold the waist under) is a standard navy/lifesaving drill (general knowledge, not from tonight's search).
