- Narrated horror stories (creepypasta / Reddit-style narration) remain the most consistent horror format on TikTok; best performers use dramatic narration, compact story, fast setup-to-scare. (virlo.ai horror storytelling agent; hooked.so horror trends)
- POV hooks over-perform: ~27.6% of tracked POV hooks became outliers vs ~19% average (Virlo viral hooks data, TikTok-focused).
- First 3 seconds decide retention on Shorts (pollo.ai, vidIQ hook guides) -> open in the middle of the danger, not with backstory.
- TikTok trends are increasingly repeatable FORMATS rather than sounds (WERSM; SocialPilot weekly tracker Oct 12-25) -> keep our recognisable 3-part format + two-word question.
- Zombie / isolated-survivor setups keep drawing views; a common beat is learning about the outbreak via an emergency broadcast, then tension from both the infected and other survivors (Recapio recap of a zombie-apocalypse video).
