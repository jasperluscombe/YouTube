Total ~1,950 views in the first day. TikTok is the strongest platform. Part 2 got 4 comments on TikTok (comment prompts work).

## Findings
1. Average watch time is 5-10 seconds on 2-3 minute videos. Most viewers decide in the first ~10s and leave.
   This is the #1 thing to fix: the opening.
2. About a third of Part 1 viewers came back for Parts 2/3 on TikTok (606 -> ~215).
3. Very early data (one story), so treat as directional.

## Changes for the next stories (apply from the next production run)
- First 3 seconds: open on MOTION and danger (something moving or hitting/breaking in frame 1), with the
  two-word question as huge on-screen text from frame 1. No slow establishing shots, no fade-in.
- First 10 seconds: the full premise in one breath (who, where, what's wrong). Backstory comes later, if at all.
- Cut every line that doesn't move the danger forward; aim for ~120-150s per part, not more.
- Keep a visual change at least every 2-3 seconds (camera move, cut, new text card).
- Parts 2 and 3 open with a 5-second "previously" that restates the cliffhanger as a question, so they work standalone.
